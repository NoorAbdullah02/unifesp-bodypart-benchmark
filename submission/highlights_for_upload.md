# Highlights (Elsevier format: 3-5 bullets, max 85 characters each)
* Patient-level benchmark for radiograph body-part classification on public data
* Random splits and test-set thresholds inflate performance estimates measurably
* Fusion, ensembling and pseudo-labels give no significant gain over one model
* Calibration, selective prediction and an out-of-fold label audit for routing use
* MURA body-part accuracy 0.975 (95% CI 0.966-0.982); transfer to FracAtlas moderate
