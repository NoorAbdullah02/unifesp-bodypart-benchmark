# Journal shortlist: free to publish (no APC) + as high as realistic

Searched Nov 2026. **Nothing here is a guarantee of acceptance, and fees/quartiles change -- re-check the three things in section 3 on the day you submit.**
"Free" below means: publish through the **subscription (non-open-access) route**, which for hybrid journals has no article processing charge.

## 1. How "free" works (verified vs not)

| Route | What I found | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Hybrid journal, subscription route | Elsevier / IEEE EMBS / Springer hybrids let authors publish without APC and charge only if the author chooses open access (EMBS JBHI page: "hybrid journal, either Traditional or Open Access (author pays)"; an Elsevier hybrid listing: OA option ~US$2,640, "mandatory publishing costs are not charged") | verified for JBHI (page text) and one Elsevier title (library listing); **generalised to the other hybrids -- confirm on each journal's author page** |
| Research4Life APC waiver | **Bangladesh is Research4Life Group A.** Elsevier waives 100 % of the APC for FULLY open-access journals only if the corresponding author AND all co-authors are at Group-A institutions; "our waiving policy does not apply to hybrid journals"; mixed-country author lists get nothing | verified (Research4Life + Elsevier pages via search). Not needed for hybrids, useful only if you target a fully-OA journal |
| Possible extra charges | IEEE journals: over-length and voluntary page charges can apply (not verified for TMI/JBHI -- read "Information for Authors"); colour-in-print etc. | NOT verified |
| Funder / university mandates | If your institution or funder requires OA you may have to pay or use a repository (green OA, usually after an embargo) | check your own rules |

## 3. Verify on the day (10 minutes per journal)

1. **Quartile in the year you submit** -- Scimago (SJR) and/or Clarivate JCR; choose the category that your institution/degree counts (SJR and JCR quartiles differ; BSPC is a known example).
2. **Author-guidelines page text:** an explicit sentence like "no publication fee for articles published via the subscription model" (Elsevier/Springer) or the EMBS "Traditional" route.
3. **Scope and recent papers:** read 5-10 recent papers in that journal on body-part / radiograph classification; if none, ask the editor via a presubmission enquiry (many Elsevier journals accept them).

## 4. Strategy ("best" without wasting a year)

* Submit to **#1 or #2** with the benchmark + protocol story once the ablation / MURA / cross-dataset results exist.
* Escalate to **MedIA / RAI** only if a *method* result appears (fusion fix with paired-bootstrap CI excluding 0, or a clearly better cross-dataset generalisation).
* Keep a preprint on arXiv/TechRxiv/medRxiv (check each journal's preprint policy first).
* Plan for one desk reject / reject-with-review: each journal takes 1-3 months to first decision (unmeasured, varies).
